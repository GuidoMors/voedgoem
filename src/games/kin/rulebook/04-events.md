# EVENTS

All cards that don't require a technology start on the event track of the phase board.

| Technology required | Event Card |
|---|---|
|  | Seasons Change |
|  | Attrition |
| Trade Diplomacy | Lapsed Treaties |
| Archeology Science | Archeology |
| Council Science | Council |
| Tactician Science OR Subterfuge Science | Tactician |


## Seasons Change | event card
> Short description: This card is special in that it automatically activates at the start of the Event Phase. When activated it untaps all other cards, and all buildings produce their resources.
> Effect: Untap all tapped cards, except the Seasons Change card itself. Every building produces its resource. This card taps.
> Extra rules: It automatically untaps at the start of the Event Phase. 

## Attrition | event card
> Short description: All [[army|armies]] on [[unbuilt]] [[snow]] lose 1 [[strength]].
> Condition: There exists an army that will lose 1 strength when this is activated
> Effect: All armies on unbuilt snow tiles lose 1 strength. This card taps.
> Extra rules: Armies that have the core faction effect Arctic, either because they are the Wanderers or they hold their Faction Treaty, are unaffected. 

## Lapsed Treaties | event card
> Short description: All [[Trade Treaty]] cards held by players no longer connected are dissolved. All arbites decided whether the promises on [[Promise Treaty]] cards are broken.
> Condition: There exists a player who has a Trade Treaty with someone they are not connected to, or someone holds a Promise Treaty.
> Effect: Remove all Trade Treaties from Courts of players who they are not connected to.
For each Promise Treaty each arbiter of that treaty has to submit whether they believe the treaty has been broken last round. If they say yes, the giver of the treaty breaks it. This card taps.

## Archeology | event card
> Short description: A [[ruins]] is placed on a random tile.
> Condition: There exists an unbuilt tile without an army on it that is not an ocean.
> Effect: A ruins block is placed on a random unbuilt tile without an army on it that is not an ocean. This card taps.

## Council | event card
> Short description: A council is held between the players. Each player has votes equal to their population to vote on [[law card]]s. The law with the most vote is enacted for this round.
> Effect: First enacted laws from previous rounds are discarded into the law deck discard pile. Then three random law cards are drawn from the law deck. In natural order each player votes for one of the three laws. That law gains votes equal to their population. The law with the most votes is active for that round. In case of a tie, no law is enacted. All laws not enacted are discarded into the law deck discard pile. If the [[Council Science]] is in a player's court, only they may vote. If it is public, all players may vote. This card taps.

## Tactician | event card
> Short description: An auction is held for a random [[tactics card]].
> Condition: At least one of the players that can bid on a card have gold in their court.
> Effect: A random card is drawn from the [[tactics deck]]. Players bid gold in natural order. Bidding starts at 1 gold. The highest bidder claims it. This card taps.
> Extra Rules: If a tactics card is drawn, only those that know Tactician Science Technology may bid on the card. If a secret tactics card is drawn, only those that know Subterfuge Science Technology may bid on the card. You may only place higher bids than the current highest bid.
