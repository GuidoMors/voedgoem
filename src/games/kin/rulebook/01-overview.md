# Overview

In Kin, you create a fantastical world filled with people with their own unique powers, which you control by building empires and commanding forces. Each Kin has their own way of decimating, bankrupting, disrupting or otherwise defeating their opponents.

## Setup
First you setup the world board:
- placing lands like mountains and forests on the world board.
- selecting which Kin you want to play.
- placing the starting buildings of your Kin.
- drafting characters to join your court.

## Rounds & Phases
Each round goes through four phases:
- event phase: where events that affect everyone happen.
- discovery phase: where you move around ayour armies, research technologies, and barter with other players.
- expansion phase: where you build buildings and deploy armies.
- control phase: where your armies recruit, battle and harvest.
