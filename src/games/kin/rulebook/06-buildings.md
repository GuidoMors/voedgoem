# Buildings

Buildings cost resources. Pay them from your [[court]]. Some buildings produce resources. They do so during the [[Seasons Change]] [[event]]. Each [[Kin]] also has a [[faction building]] only they can [[Build]].

[[Building Blueprints]] are cards on the [[building board]] that anyone can [[Build]].


## Icons

Buildings can occupy different parts of a tile:
- [[tile building]]s are placed on top of a tile, replacing it.
- [[center building]]s are placed on the center of a tile without replacing it.
- [[slice building]]s are placed covering a slice of a tile, and are attached at one edge of a tile.
- [[path building]]s are placed connecting two centers of tiles.
- [[edge building]]s are placed in the edge between two tiles.


# Buildings Blueprints on the building board at the start of the game

## Village | building blueprint | ⬢ | 🏘
> Resource cost: 2 lumber, 2 food
> Build Power cost: 3
> Garrison: 2
> Build conditions: On plains or snow, with at least a distance of 3 tiles from any other tile building.
> Effects: Worth 1 [[population]]

## City | building blueprint | ⬢ | 🏙
> Resource cost: 2 stone, 2 gold
> Build Power cost: 4
> Garrison: 4
> Build conditions: Build on top of a village and replace it.
> Effects: Worth 2 [[population]]

## Worksite | building blueprint | ⨺ | ⛏️,🪏,🪚
> Resource cost: 2 basic resources
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Inside a tile building. An adjacent tile must be farmland, forest, or a mountain.
> Effects:
> - If the adjacent tile is farmland: The Worksite is a Farm 🪏 that produces 1 food.
> - If the adjacent tile is a mountain: The Worksite is a Quarry ⛏️ that produces 1 stone.
> - If the adjacent tile is forest: The Worksite is a Lumberyard 🪚 that produces 1 lumber.

## Barracks | building blueprint | ⨺ | ⚔️
> Resource cost: 2 lumber, 1 stone
> Build Power cost: 2
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Inside a tile building, on plains or snow.
> Effects: Adds 2 garrison to the tile building and increases your army limit by 1. Armies can Deploy and Recruit here.

## Road | building blueprint | +
> Resource cost: 1 stone
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Must not touch the ocean.
> Effects: Movement across costs ½ speed. Roads are not owned by anyone, but can be used as a connection building to another building you own for the purposes of placing new buildings.

> Resource cost: 1 stone
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: None.
