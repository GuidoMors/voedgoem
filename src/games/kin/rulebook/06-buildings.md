# Buildings

Buildings cost resources. Pay them from your [[court]]. Some buildings produce resources. They do so during the [[Seasons Change]] [[event]]. Each [[Kin]] also has a [[faction building]] only they can [[Build]].

[[Building Blueprints]] are cards on the [[building board]] that anyone can [[Build]].


## Icons

Buildings can occupy different parts of a tile:
- [[tile building]]s are placed on top of a tile, replacing it.
- [[center building]]s are placed on the center of a tile without replacing it.
- [[slice building]]s are placed covering a slice of a tile, and are attached at one edge of a tile.
- [[path building]]s are placed connecting two centers of tiles.
- [[edge building]]s are placed in the edge between two tiles.

## Village | building blueprint | ⬢ | 🏘
> Resource cost: 2 [[lumber]], 2 [[food]]
> Build Power cost: 3
> Garrison: 2
> Build conditions: On [[plains]] or [[snow]], with at least a distance of 3 tiles from any other [[tile building]].
> Effects: Worth 1 [[population]]

## City | building blueprint | ⬢ | 🏙
> Resource cost: 2 [[stone]], 2 [[gold]]
> Build Power cost: 4
> Garrison: 4
> Build conditions: Upgrade from a [[village]]
> Effects: Worth 2 [[population]]
// Upgrade in this context means you select a village and turn it into a City instead.

## Worksite | building blueprint | ⨺ | 🪓,⛏️,🪏
> Resource cost: 2 [[basic resource]]s
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Inside a [[tile building]]. An adjacent tile must be [[farmland]], [[forest]], or [[mountain]].
> Effects: Produces 1 [[basic resource]] of the tile its adjacent to.
// If the adjacent tile is farmland: The Worksite is a Farm 🪏 that produces 1 food.
//If the adjacent tile is a mountain: The Worksite is a Quarry ⛏️ that produces 1 stone.
// If the adjacent tile is forest: The Worksite is a Lumberyard 🪓 that produces 1 lumber.
// Deep Ones' Worksites are called Shrines 🪬. On ocean tiles, Underwater Cult makes them produce their tile building's dedicated resource.

## Barracks | building blueprint | ⨺ | ⚔️
> Resource cost: 2 [[lumber]], 1 [[stone]]
> Build Power cost: 2
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: -
> Effects: Adds 2 [[garrison]] to the [[tile building]] and increases your [[army limit]] by 1. [[army|Armies]] can [[Deploy]] and [[Recruit]] here.

## Road | building blueprint | +
> Resource cost: 1 [[stone]]
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Must not cross into [[ocean]].
> Effects: Movement across costs ½ speed. 
// Roads are not owned by anyone, but can be used as a connection building to another building you own for the purposes of placing new buildings.

## University | building blueprint | ⨺ | 📖
> Resource cost: 2 [[stone]], 2 [[gold]]
> Build Power cost: 3
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Limit of 1 per [[tile building]].
> Effects: Produces 1 [[gear]].

## Market | building blueprint | ⨺ | ⚖️
> Resource cost: 1 [[lumber]], 1 [[food]]
> Build Power cost: 2
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: An adjacent edge must be a [[river]]. Limit 1 per [[tile building]].
> Effects: Produces 1 [[gold]].

## Smith | building blueprint | ⨺ | 🔨
> Resource cost: 1 [[stone]]
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: None.
> Effects: Lowers build power required to [[Build]] or [[Demolish]] buildings on this tile by 1, to a minimum of 0.

## Harbor | building blueprint | ⨺ | ⚓
> Resource cost: 2 [[stone]], 2 [[lumber]]
> Build Power cost: 3
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Adjacent to [[ocean]] or a [[river]]. Limit 1 per [[tile building]].
> Effects: Is used to [[Embark]] and to connect players for the [[Trade Treaty]]. A Harbor connects through a continuous route of rivers and ocean tiles to another player's tile building; the destination does not need a Harbor.

## Mage College | building blueprint | ⨺ | 🟣
> Resource cost: 2 [[stone]], 2 [[gold]]
> Build Power cost: 3
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: One per [[city]]. Must be built in a [[city]].
> Effects: Produces 1 [[mana]].

## Wall | building blueprint | / 
> Resource cost: 1 [[stone]]
> Build Power cost: 1
> Garrison: 0
> Build conditions: Can not be build adjacent to an [[ocean]].
> Effects: Prevents you from [[Move|Moving]] or [[Battle|Battling]] across it, except if you own it and there is a [[Road]] across it.
