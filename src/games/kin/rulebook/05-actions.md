## Activating an action card

1. [[Tap]] one of your [[untap]]ped [[character]]s or untapped [[action card]]s whose [[archetype]] matches the [[phase]], and pay any costs associated with the [[action card]].
2. The rules as written on the action card activate.
3. Then your turn passes to the next player in [[natural order]].

You may activate cards of the current phase on the phase board and cards of the current phase in your court.

## Move | action card
> Short Description: Move an [[army]] to other tiles. The power of the character increases how far you can Move.
> Phase: Discovery
> Condition: You have armies.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Discovery Character/Activation Card. Select an army you own.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: For this Move you have speed equal to the power of the card used to activate this action. You may spend Speed to move the selected army to adjacent tiles for their [[tile speed cost]]. When you run out of speed, or when you choose to, you stop moving the Army.
> Extra Rules: You may bring 1 [[relic block]] or 1 [[treasure block]] along with the army each tile you Move. You may not move your Army off a tile if there is an adjacent army of another player without that player's permission. With an access treaty that permission is not necessary: you get the permission.

## Research | action card
> Short Description: Use [[gears]] to research a [[technology]] from the [[technology board]].
> Phase: Discovery
> Condition: There is a technology you could research.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Discovery Character/Activation Card. Select a technology on the research track of the technology board.
> Payment: The cost of the Technology in gears, Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Claim the selected technology, and claim any cards that that technology tells you to claim.

## Publicize | action card
> Short Description: Publicize a [[technology]] in any player's court. Publicizing your own technology costs no [[gear]].
> Phase: Discovery
> Condition: There is a technology you could publicise.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Discovery Character/Activation Card. Select a technology in any player's court.
> Payment: Tap the selected character/activation card. If the selected technology belongs to another player, pay its gear cost; publicizing your own technology costs no gear.
> Effect: Make the selected technology [[public]].

## Barter | action card
> Short Description: Trade resources, treaties, characters, and other cards with another player.
> Phase: Discovery
> Condition: none.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Discovery Character/Activation Card. Select a player. Then select a certain amount of bartering goods from your court. Then select a certain amount of bartering goods from the selected player's court.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: The selected player may accept or decline the offer. If accepted, move the items selected in your court to their court and the items selected in their court to your court. If declined, no items are transferred.
> Extra Rules: You can only barter your own trade treaties. You cannot barter the trade treaties you have received from others. A barter is successful only if the selected player accepts it.

## Dissolve | action card
> Short Description: Dissolve a [[treaty]] you hold of another player.
> Phase: Discovery
> Condition: There is a treaty you could dissolve.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Discovery Character/Activation Card. Select a treaty in your court given to you by another player.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Dissolve the selected treaty. The treaty’s consequences apply as written on the treaty.
> Extra Rules: Returning a treaty does not count as you breaking it.

## Build | action card
> Short Description: Place a building on the [[world board]] by paying their construction cost. You can build on a tile with an army, or on tile adjacent to another building.
> Phase: Expansion
> Condition: none.
> On trigger: Select an untapped Expansion Character/Activation Card. Select a building blueprint in your court or on the building board. Select a tile/edge to place the building on that meets the Building Conditions. Select enough expansion characters / expansion activation cards with enough power to satisfy the build power of the selected building.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards, paying the construction cost of the building.
> Effect: Place a building of the selected building blueprint on the selected tile/edge. You own that building.
> Extra Rules: You cannot build on a slot that is already built.

## Deploy | action card
> Short Description: Place a new [[army]] on a [[Barracks]] or other building you can recruit on.
> Phase: Expansion
> On trigger: Select an untapped Expansion Character/Activation Card. Select a building you own on which you may Deploy armies.
> Condition: You have a building on which you can Deploy. You have less armies than your [[army limit]],
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Put an army of strength 1 on the tile with the building.

## Demolish | action card
> Short Description: Remove a building on a tile with an [[army]]. (can also be used on Walls with 4 power)
> Phase: Expansion
> Condition: You have a building that can be demolished, or you have an army adjacent to a wall.
> On trigger: Selecting untapped Expansion Characters/Activation Cards with a total of at least 3 build power. Then select a building you own with an army you own on the same tile, or select a wall that is adjacent to your army (walls from other players allowed). If a wall was selected and the selected expansion character/activation card has less than 4 power, select expansion characters/activation cards until 4 power is hit.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: Remove the selected building from play. If the army was owned by another player, this counts towards breaking treaties.

## Reclaim | action card
> Short Description: Reclaim a [[treasure block]] or [[relic block]] on a tile building.
Phase: Expansion
> Condition: You have an army that can Reclaim.
> On trigger: Select an untapped Expansion Character/Activation Card. Select a tile building you own with a relic block or treasure block on the tile.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Remove all relic and treasure blocks from that tile. You gain 3 basic resources of your choice per treasure and 1 relic per relic block removed.

## Revoke | action card
> Short description: Spend 5 power to discard an enacted law.
> Phase: Expansion
> Condition: There is an enacted law. You have a 5 expansion power untapped.
> On trigger: Select an untapped Expansion Character/Activation Card. Select a law card. Select activation cards and character cards that add up to 5 power.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: The selected law card is discard.

## Battle | action card
> Short Description: Start a battle with an [[army]] against another army. Both sides roll dice to deal [[hit]] or [[block]].
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You own an army that is adjacent to a tile building or center building or army owned by another player.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. Select an army that is adjacent to a tile building or center building or army owned by another player. Select a tile adjacent to the selected army that contains either a tile building or center building or army.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character card.
> Effect: 
> Step: **Generals.** You are the attacker. The other player is the defender. The character you tapped for the Battle action is the [[attacking general]] The defender as a response may tap a Control character in their court, if they do this Control character is the [[defending general]].
> Step: **Pool Strength.** Calculate the pools of strength. Each side has their own pool of strength. Add up the following for each side:
> - the power of each side's general
> - the strength of the army each side's tile.
> - the [[garrison]] of the building on the defender's tile (if any). The attacker does not add any garrison from any buildings on their tile.
> - any possible abilities from characters in court that add strength to pools.
> Step: **Roll dice.** Each side rolls 1 dice per point of strength in their respective pools. They may choose any combination of combat dice available to them. At the start of the game only the strategic dice are available to players, but more can be unlocked through technologies.
> Step: **Resolve Dice.**
>- Add self hits to the hit total of your opponent.
> - Subtract the defender’s blocks from the attacker’s hits. Subtract the attacker’s blocks from the defender’s hits.
> - Remaining hits are dealt to armies first. Each hit reduces an army’s strength by 1. An army at 0 strength is [[disband]]ed.
> - If hits remain after the defender’s armies, the building on the tile is destroyed if the remaining hits are at least its garrison. (A building with no garrison and no army on it is destroyed immediately, with no dice required).
> Step: **Ambush.** Each side checks separately whether the opposing general can be taken hostage. The defending general can be taken hostage if the defender's tile is unbuilt; the attacking general can be taken hostage if the attacker's tile is unbuilt. The side attempting to take a general hostage must have dealt at least 1 unblocked hit and rolled an [[ambush]] total greater than that general's power. If both sides meet these conditions, both generals become hostages.
> Extra Rules: Buildings are destroyed, not captured. Ownership of buildings cannot be transferred through Battle.

## Recruit | action card
> Short Description: Increase the [[strength]] of an [[army]] on a [[Barracks]] or other buildings that can recruit.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You own an army with less strength than your maximum strength on a building you own that can Recruit.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. Select an army with less strength than your maximum strength on a building you own that can Recruit.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards. Pay 2 food.
> Effect: Add 1 strength to the selected army.
> Extra Rules: The maximum strength of an army is 3 by default. It can be increased by the Technology: Greater Army Strength.

## Reinforce | action card
> Short Description: Exchange [[strength]] between two adjacent [[army|armies]].
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You own two adjacent armies and both aren't maximum strength.
> On trigger: 0. select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. 1, Select an army you own. 2. select an army adjacent to the selected army
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: Exchange any amount of strength between two selected armies.
> Extra rules: Neither army may go above the maximum strength of an army.

## Harvest | action card
> Short Description: Put an [[army]] to work on a [[farmland]], [[forest]] or [[mountain]], gaining a resource of that type. The army becomes [[overexhausted]]: it cannot [[Move]], [[Battle]], or [[Harvest]] this round and next round.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You own an army on a farmland, forest, or mountain.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. Select an army you own on a farmland, forest, or mountain.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: Gain 1 resource of that tile's type (farmland gives food, mountain gives stone, forest gives lumber). Put the overexhausted status on the selected army.
> Extra rules: The army is overexhausted for the rest of this round. At the next [[Seasons Change]], it becomes exhausted; it remains exhausted for that round and is cleared at the following Seasons Change.

## Explore | action card
> Short description: Explore a [[ruins]] with an [[army]] to find a [[warp]], [[treasure block]] or [[relic block]].
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You own an army on an ruins.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card.  Select an army you own on an ruins.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: A d6 is rolled and the ruins block is removed.
> On 1 to 2 the ruins is a warp: you immediately claim 2 mana.
> On 3 to 4 the ruins is a treasure: put a treasure block on that tile.
> On 5 to 6: the ruins is a relic: put a relic block on that tile.

## Embark | action card
> Short description: Move onto an [[ocean]] with an [[army]] on a [[Harbor]] or other building that can embark. The army is then [[embarked]]: it can move over oceans.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You own an army on a harbor with an adjacent ocean tile.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. Select an army you own on a harbor. Then select an adjacent ocean tile.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: Move the selected army onto the selected tile. give it the embarked status.
