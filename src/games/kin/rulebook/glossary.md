# Glossary

## kin | ❖
The faction a player leads. Each Kin has [[faction effect]]s, faction building, and characters. See a list of the kin under [[natural order]].
## tap | ↷ | plain
Turn a card sideways. A tapped card cannot be tapped again until [[untap]]ped. [[Character]]s, [[relic]]s, [[treaty|treaties]] and [[event card]]s can be tapped.
## untap | ↶ | plain
Turn a [[tap]]ped card upright. Untapped cards cannot be untapped.
## pass | 🠞
End your turn without activating a card.

## tile type
The tile types are [[snow]], [[plains]], [[desert]], [[farmland]], [[mountain]], [[forest]], [[ocean]], [[tile building]].
## claim
When it says to claim something, put it in your [[court]]. You own it. It enters [[untap]]ped.
## phase | ◐
One of four steps of a round: [[Event]], [[Discovery]], [[Expansion]], [[Control]]. They are tracked on the [[phase board]] by the [[phase marker]] in the top right.
## phase marker | ★
White star token showing the current [[phase]] on the [[phase board]].
## round | ⟳
Going through all [[phase]]s from start to finish.
## chapter
A stage that the game exists in with special rules and a [[victory condition]]. After the last [[phase]], the [[control]] phase, this victory condition is checked for a winner. In a long game, if there is a winner they may pick the next chapter. If they win twice in a row, they win the entire game.
## leader | ♛
The winner of the previous [[chapter]]. During the first chapter, the game has no leader.
## archetype | ☗
[[Discovery]] (blue), [[Expansion]] (yellow), [[Control]] (red) or [[Universal]] (all three, dashed). Universal counts as every archetype.
## discovery
One of the four phases. Its color is blue. The phase revolves around exploring and researching. Discovery [[action card]]s can be [[activate]]d in this phase.
## expansion
One of the four phases. Its color is yellow. The phase revolves around building and spending resources. Expansion [[action cards]] can be [[activate]]d in this phase.
## control
One of the four phases. Its color is red. The phase revolves around armies and combat. Control [[action card]]s can be [[activate]]d in this phase.
## event
One of the four phases. Its color is purple. In this phase [[event card]]s are [[activate]]d.
## character
A card with an [[archetype]], [[power]] and an [[ability]]. You may tap character cards to [[activate]] a matching [[action card]] of that [[archetype]] during the matching archetype [[phase]].
## power | ⬥
Property of [[character]]s. Power is printed top right of a character. The power of a character is relevant for certain [[action card]]s: like [[Battle]], [[Move]], [[Build]].
## army | ⛋
A circle with a number on the center of a tile. They are used to [[Battle]]. Two armies cannot share a tile.
## strength | ⚑
The number on the circle of an [[army]]. It is used in [[Battle]]. Default maximum is 3. When an army reaches 0 army strength, they are [[disband]]ed.
## garrison | ⛨
Strength a building adds to its side’s [[strength pool]] when its tile is attacked. The building is destroyed when leftover hits are at least equal to its garrison.
## general
[[Character]] used in a [[Battle]].
## attacking general
The [[character]] tapped to take the [[Battle]] action.
## defending general
A [[character]] the defender may tap in response to a [[Battle]]. Optional.
## disband
Remove an army from the [[world board]].
## ambush | 🗡
Total of the ambush [[combat dice]] rolled by a side. If the side dealt at least 1 unblocked hit to an unbuilt tile, its ambush total takes the opposing [[general]] [[hostage]] if the total is greater than that general's [[power]].
## hostage
A locked character in an [[court]]. It cannot [[activate]] its [[ability]] or be [[tap]]ped, but it can be used in [[Barter]]. When it returns to its owner’s court it unlocks.
## lock
A locked card cannot be [[activate]]d or [[tap]]ped.
## unlocked
Cards are unlocked by default. It means that they can be [[activate]]d at tapped.
## basic resource | ⠶ | basic
[[Gold]], [[lumber]], [[stone]] or [[food]].
## special resource
[[Gear]], [[mana]], and [[relic]].
## gold | ⏺ | gold
A [[basic resource]] kept in your [[court]]. Associated with [[river]]s.
## lumber | █ | lum
A [[basic resource]] kept in your [[court]]. Associated with [[forest]]s.
## stone | ▲ | stone
A [[basic resource]] kept in your [[court]]. Associated with [[mountains]].
## food | ❀ | food
A [[basic resource]] kept in your [[court]]. Associated with [[farmland]].
## gear | ⚙ | gear
A [[special resource]] kept in your [[court]]. Paid for [[technology|technologies]].
## mana | ✦ | mana
A [[special resource]] kept in your [[court]]. Paid for magical [[ability|abilities]].
## relic | 𐚬 | rel
During your turn in any phase, click the relic icon in your court to tap a chosen number of untapped relics and gain that many of one chosen basic resource. This does not end your turn. Relics untap at [[Seasons Change]]. Only untapped relics can be bartered. Relic counts show (tapped/total).
A [[special resource]] kept in your [[court]]. You can tap it to gain a [[basic resource]] of your choice. It can be tapped in any [[phase]]. It is special in that it does not end your turn when you tap it.
## mountain | ⬢ | tile-mountain
A tile type placed on the world board. It is associated with [[stone]]. It is a resource tile type, together with [[forest]] and [[farmland]].
## forest | ⬢ | tile-forest
A tile type placed on the world board. It is associated with [[lumber]]. It is a resource tile type, together with [[mountain]] and [[farmland]].
## farmland | ⬢ | tile-farmland
A tile type placed on the world board. It is associated with [[lumber]]. It is a resource tile type, together with [[mountain]] and [[forest]].
## plains | ⬢ | tile-plains
A tile type placed on the world board. It's climate is the tiles that are not in the middle, top, or bottom 2 rows. It is a common tile type, together with [[snow]] and [[desert]].
## snow | ⬢ | tile-snow
A tile type placed on the world board. It's climate is the top and bottom two rows. It is a common tile type, together with [[plains]] and [[desert]].
## desert | ⬢ | tile-desert
A tile type placed on the world board. It's climate is in the middle two rows. It is a common tile type, together with [[plains]] and [[snow]].
## ocean | ⬢ | tile-ocean
The tile type that all tiles on the world board start as.
## ruins | ♜
A block on a tile which an [[army]] can [[Explore]]. Ruins cannot be [[Move]]d. A tile with ruins on it is not considered unbuilt. No tile building or center building can be [[Build]] on it.
## relic block
A [[ruins]] result. A block on a tile which can be collected using [[Reclaim]] to gain 1 [[relic]]. Using the [[Move]] action [[army|armies]] can take relic blocks to new tiles. Any building can be built on a tile with a relic block.
## treasure block
A [[ruins]] result. A block on a tile which can be collected using [[Reclaim]] to gain 3 [[basic resource]]s. Using the [[Move]] action armies can take treasure blocks to new tiles. Any building can be built on a tile with a treasure block.
## treaty
A card given to another player in [[Barter]]. Breaking a treaty means violating it with consequences. [[Dissolve]] means ending it voluntarily.
## population | ⓟ
Provided by buildings. [[Village]] adds 1. A [[city]] adds 2. [[Faction building]] may also increase it.
## ocean | ⬢ | tile-ocean
Empty tiles. Impassable unless [[Embark]]ed.
## army limit | ≐
The limit of armies you may own at a time. Default is 1. [[Barracks]] adds 1.[[Faction building]] may also increase it.
## block | ⛊
Rolled on [[combat dice]] in [[Battle]] It cancels out one [[hit]] of the opponents side.
## hit  | ✸
Rolled on [[combat dice]] in [[Battle]]. Any [[unblocked hit]]s are dealt to your opponent.
## self hit | 🌢
A [[hit]] that is added to the hits of the opposing side in a [[Battle]]. They are found on [[combat dice]].
## combat dice
Rolled during a [[Battle]] for each point in the [[strength pool]]. There are four kinds of dice: Strategic, Defensive, Aggresive, Ambush. Only Strategic is unlocked by default. The others require [[technology|technologies]].
## unblocked hits
[[Hit]]s that remain after [[block]]s. They reduce [[army]] [[strength]] first, then they can destroy a [[building]] on the attacked tile.
## court
Where you hold your cards and resources that you own.
## general
The character a side taps for a [[Battle]]. Its [[power]] joins the [[strength pool]].
## strength pool
Added up in Battle as follows:
- the power of each side's [[general]].
- the [[strength]] of the [[army]] each side's tile.
- the [[garrison]] of the building on the defender's tile (if any). The attacker does not add any garrison from any buildings on their tile.
## embarked | 🜰
A [[status]] of an army provided by [[Embark]], where movement to ocean is unblocked at a cost of ½ speed. An army loses this status when they move onto a tile type that is not ocean.
## status
An effect active on an army. Examples are embarked and webbed.
## exhausted | 𐄂
A [[status]] of an army where they are not allowed to be Move, Reinforce, Harvest or Battle this round. Clears during the [[Seasons Change]] event. Exhausted armies can still be attacked, and then function as normal.
## overexhausted | ⛒
A [[status]] of an army where they are not allowed to Move, Reinforce, Harvest or Battle for the rest of the round in which it is applied. At the next [[Seasons Change]], it becomes [[exhausted]] for the following round. Exhausted armies can still be attacked, and then function as normal.
## warp | ⌘ | purple
A [[ruins]] result. The explorer gains 2 mana.
## virtue | ✜ | yellow
[[Kin]]-specific counter of [[Pride of Lumon]]: it is equal to the number of its [[treaty|treaties]] other players hold. Cannot be spent or [[Barter]]ed.
## influence | ⍟ | brown
[[Kin]]-specific resource of [[Dynasty of Hanshu]]. Cannot be [[Barter]]ed.
## tradegoods | 🗃︎ | blue
[[Kin]]-specific resource of [[Seven Streams Syndicate]]. When [[Barter]]ed, it is assigned one basic resource type and the receiver gets that resource.
## technology
A card. You can research it for a cost in [[gear]]s. When you research it from the research track of the [[technology board]], put it in your [[court]]. When you [[Publicize]] it from another player's court, put it on the public track of the technology board.
## public
A public card is useable by everyone. They lie for example on the public track of the [[technology board]], the [[building board]], or phase board.
## produce
When a card or building says it produces, it produces that amount of resources during the [[Seasons Change]] event.
## tactics deck
The deck of [[tactics card]]s and [[secret tactics card]]s. A tactics card that is played, or a tactics card that is not bid on, is put in the discard pile of the tactics deck. When it is empty and a card must be drawn, shuffle the tactics card discard pile into a new deck. If no tactics cards are in the discard pile, no card is drawn.
## secret tactics card
The secret tactic cards are shuffled into the tactics deck upon someone researching the [[Subterfuge Science]] [[technology]]. The number of secret tactics cards is equal to the number of [[subterfuge card]]s that exist. It is bid on during the [[Tactician]] Event. Only players that know the [[Subterfuge Science]] may bid on it. When you win a bid for a secret tactics card, you draw a card from the subterfuge deck.
## subterfuge deck
The deck of [[subterfuge card]]s. You may draw from it when you win a bid for a [[secret tactics card]]. The secret tactics card goes to the discard pile of the tactics deck. When a card must be drawn from the subterfuge deck, and the deck is empty. Shuffle the discard pile of the subterfuge cards into the subterfuge deck and draw from it. If no subterfuge cards are in the discard pile, no card is drawn.
## character deck
The neutral [[character]]s and characters, from the Kin played at the table, which weren't picked during setup.
## world board
The board containing all the tiles, [[river]]s, buildings, and [[army|armies]]. No cards are put on the world board.
## phase board
The board showing the current [[phase]]. It has four tracks for each phase: [[Event]], [[Discovery]], [[Expansion]], [[Control]]. On the tracks the [[action card]]s and [[event card]]s lie that are [[public]]. The [[phase marker]] above one of the tracks indicates which phase it is. Only those cards can be activated.
## technology board
On the technology board lie the [[technology|technologies]]. It has 2 tracks: a research track and a public track. Technlogies in the research track aren't know by anyone, but can be [[Research]]ed. Technologies in the public track are known by everyone, as they are [[public]].
## building board
It has two tracks, a public track, and locked track. In the public track lie all the [[public]] [[building blueprint]] cards. In the locked track lie all the building blueprint cards that are not known by anyone. They come into play when their [[technology]] is [[Researche]]d.
## ability
Every [[character]] has an ability. There are 2 types of abilities: [[tap ability]] and [[passive ability]].
## tap ability | ↷ 
A tap ability is [[activate]]d by clicking the ruletext of the [[ability]] on a [[character]] in your [[court]] in the [[phase]] of that character's [[archetype]]. When you activate it, the character [[tap]]s, and the effects of the card resolve. After resolving the effects of the ability, just like activating an [[action card]], you end your turn.
## passive ability | ◫ 
A passive ability is an [[ability]] which can not be [[activate]]d by clicking the ruletext of the ability. Instead it triggers in response to another action being activated. Some passive abilities trigger automatically (they have no cost or conditions). Some passive abilities show a pop-up on your screen asking you if you want to activate it (they have a cost or a condition).
## connected
Two players are connected if one of the following is true: they own buildings adjacent to eachother.
Either player's [[Harbor]] can reach a [[tile building]] owned by the other player through a continuous route of [[river]]s and [[ocean]] tiles. The route may pass between rivers and ocean tiles where they meet. The destination does not need a Harbor.
## tile speed cost
Relevant for the [[Move]] action. Each tile type has its own speed cost:
| Tile | Cost |
|---|---|
| Plains, snow, farmland, forest | 1 |
| ⬢ or ◼ you own or allowed access | 1 |
| Desert | 2 |
| Mountain | 3 |
| Moving across a river without a road | +1 cost |
| Moving across a road | ½ |
| Ocean | blocks movement by default, ½ if [[Embark]]ed |
| ⬢ or ◼ you are not allowed access | blocks movement |

A road overrides the terrain cost of the tile moved to, and crossing a river on a road does not add the river cost. Faction effects override default tile costs.
## tile building | ⬢
This building type covers a tile fully. It no longer counts as being that [[tile type]] until the building tile is removed. [[Armie]]s can be moved onto building tiles.
## center building | ◼
Sits in the middle of a tile. The tile still counts as being that [[tile type]], the center building does not override it. [[Armies can be moved onto this tile]]. Center buildings cannot be build on [[tile building]]s.
## slice building | ⨺
Placed on top of a [[tile building]], attached to one of its edges. On the outside they are attached to another tile, which is the adjacent tile. An edge is adjacent to a slice building if its attached edge is adjacent to the attached edge. When a rule of a slice building speaks of "this tile" that means the tile that the slice building is placed over.
## cross building | +
A building placed between the centers of two adjacent tiles across an edge. It counts as being on both tiles, meaning that all 8 tiles count as adjacent to the cross building. Cross buildings are not blocked by another other building types. They can be build on across edge buildings, like walls and rivers, and they can be build. They can be build on the same tile as tile buildings and center buildings.
## edge building | / 
A building placed in an edge between two tiles. All four tiles the edge touches count as adjacent. [[River]]s are also placed as /, but they do not count as buildings.
## access
By default other players have no access to tiles with your [[army|armies]] and buildings on it. You can give other players access through an [[Access Treaty]].
## bartering goods
The following cards and resources are considered bartering goods:
- [[Character]]s
- [[Basic Resource]]s ([[gold]], [[lumber]], [[food]], [[stone]])
- [[Special Resource]]s ([[relic]]s, [[mana]], [[gear]]s)
- [[Tactics Card]]s
- [[treaty|treaties]]
## building blueprint
A card that shows how to construct a building. They are either [[public]] on the [[building board]]: meaning anyone can build them, or in someone's [[court]]: only they can build them.
## faction building
Each [[Kin]] has a special building that only they can build found on their Kin page. Faction buildings cannot become [[public]].
## faction effect
Each [[Kin]] has their own effects called faction effects found on their Kin page. They function like an [[ability]]. Faction effects cannot become [[public]] (except through the Faction Treaty).
## core faction effect
Each [[Kin]] has one [[faction effect]] called the core faction effect. This effect can be given through others by [[Barter]]ing the [[Faction Treaty]].
## tile distance
Some rules may refer to a distance between tiles. Tiles that are adjacent are a distance of 1 away. Each further tile is another 1 distance away. The distance between tiles is the shortest distance in steps to that tile.
## starting setup
Each [[Kin]] has a starting setup as detailed on their Kin page.
## activate
Cards with ruletext on them can be activated to have that ruletext resolve. Activating a card can require payment, like resources or tapping a card.
## unbuilt
A tile is unbuilt if it has no buildings on it.
## action card
Cards on the [[discovery]], [[expansion]], [[control]] track of the [[phase board]]. They can be activated by tapping a [[character]] or [[activation card]] of the same [[archetype]].
## activation card
Like a [[character]] has [[power]] and an [[archetype]]. An action card can be [[tap]]ped to [[activate]] an [[action card]] of the same [[archetype]] during the same [[phase]].
## event card
Cards on the [[event]] track of the [[phase board]]. When activated the event card [[tap]]s, they cannot be activated again until they are [[untap]]ped during the next [[Seasons Change]].
## law deck
The deck of [[law card]]s. When a card should be drawn from the law deck but it is empty, shuffle the law deck discard pile and draw from there.
