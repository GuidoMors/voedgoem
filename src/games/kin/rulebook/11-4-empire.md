# Empire | Chapter


## Rules: 
The leader is the Emperor.


## Chapter components: 
Authority: A resource. Each player starts with 0 Authority. Authority cannot be bartered. A vassal's authority counts towards their Lord for victory conditions, just like all resources are. Its symbol is ✊.
The City States: A neutral player. They don't take turns and don't have a court. If any of their sources are Battled, they roll a strategic dice for each strength gathered in their strength pool.
Neutral Villages: In natural order each player adds a neutral village to the world board.  When it is the Emperor's turn, they put 3 neutral villages on the world board instead of 1. If no tile is available to place a neutral village, the Emperor chooses a village or city from another player to become a neutral village. All these neutral villages are owned by the City States.
Neutral Armies: Put an Army of 4 strength owned by the City States on each Neutral Village.
Neutral Cities: Neutral Cities can be upgraded from neutral Villages usign the Expand the Empire move. None are yet present at the start of this Chapter.

Cards (public): Unify, Surpress, Demagogue
Cards (owned by the leader): Neutral City Blueprint

## Neutral Village | building blueprint | ⬢ | 🏘
> Resource cost: cannot be build
> Build Power cost: cannot be build
> Garrison: 2
> Build conditions: On plains or snow, with at least a distance of 3 tiles from any tile building.
> Effects: Worth 1 [[population]]. Produces 1 authority. No buildings can be built inside of this tile building.

## Neutral City | building blueprint | ⬢ | 🏙
> Resource cost: 2 stone, 2 gold
> Build Power cost: 4
> Garrison: 4
> Build conditions: Build on top of a village and replace it.
> Effects: Worth 2 [[population]]. Produce 2 authority. No buildings can be built inside of this tile building.

## Unify | action card
> Short description: If you own [[army|armies] with more than double the [[strength]] adjacent to a neutral [[village]] or neutral [[city]] that you do not own, you may unify it: take ownership of the neutral village or city and the army standing on it.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: there is a neutral village or city of which you have armies adjacent to it with more than double the strength than on the neutral village or city
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. Select a neutral village or city that you do not own of which you have armies adjacent to it with more than double the strength than on the neutral village or city
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Take ownership of the selected neutral village or city and the army standing on it.

## Surpress | action card
> Short description: Spend 1 [[Authority]] to increase the strength of an [[army]] on a [[Barracks]] or a building on which you can [[Recruit]].
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You have at least 1 Authority. There is an army on a Barracks or a building on which you can Recruit.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Character/Activation Card (of any archetype). Select an army on a Barracks or a building on which you can Recruit.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card. Spend 1 Authority.
> Effect: Increase the strength of the selected army by 1.

## Demagogue | effect card
> Short description: When you destroy a [[neutral village]] or [[neutral city]] through [[Battle]], gain 3 [[Authority]].
> Condition: a neutral village or city is destroyed by you in this Battle
> Trigger: Battle, when it has fully resolved
> Effect: Gain 3 Authority.


## Victory Condition: 
The winner is declared when all villages and cities are under control of players.
The winner is the player with the most authority. If there is a tie in most authority, the winner is the Emperor (even if they don't have the most authority).


## Cleanup: 
When this Chapter ends, remove all chapter components.
