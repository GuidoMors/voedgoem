# Commonwealth | Chapter

Color: green

## Rules: 
All players start as Members of the commonwealth. The leader of this chapter is the Leader of the Commonwealth. Members of the Commonwealth can wage war with eachother without losing their membership.
Discard the currently enacted law, then remove the regular law deck from play.

[[Council Science]] is not publicized by this chapter. Normal technology requirements for participating in [[Council]] still apply. Eligible members of either party may vote, but Commonwealth laws only benefit Commonwealth members. [[Revoke]] can discard an enacted Commonwealth law.

Vassals may belong to a different party from their Lord, count separately for party membership, and may become party leaders. Normal vassal victory rules still apply: a vassal cannot win, and their gold counts towards their Lord's gold for victory conditions. Comparisons for [[Overthrow]] and [[Oppose]] use personal gold and population, without adding a vassal's totals.

Changing a party leader does not change the chapter leader for collapse or campaign rules. [[Cautious Schemer]] remains with its original owner, even if they later join the Opposition.


## Chapter components: 
Commonwealth board: it has two tracks: the commonwealth counter (it has 10 steps), and the law track.
Commonwealth marker: it starts with the value 1 and on the first step of the commonwealth counter.
Commonwealth laws: For the Council event the Commonwealth Law cards are now used instead. The effects of commonwealth cards only apply to members of the Commonwealth.
Cards (public): Oppose, Overthrow, Commonwealth Donation
Cards (owned by the leader): Cautious Schemer

## Commonwealth Donation | event card
> Short description: Each member of the Commonwealth may donate [[gold]]. If total donations meet the value on the Commonwealth marker, move the marker 1 step right (stopping at step 10) and set its new value to the total donated, up to 20. If donations are less than the marker's value, reduce its value by the difference, to a minimum of 1. Half of the donated gold, rounded down, goes to the Leader of the Commonwealth. If the Commonwealth has fewer members than the Opposition, no donation takes place and the marker moves 1 step left, stopping at step 1.
> Effect: If the Commonwealth has at least as many members as the Opposition, each member of the Commonwealth, in natural order, may choose how much gold in their court they want to donate. After all players have had 1 chance to donate, do the following:
> - If the total gold donated is greater than or equal to the value on the Commonwealth marker, move the marker 1 step right, stopping at step 10, and set its new value to the total gold donated (to a maximum of 20).
> - If the total gold donated is less than the value on the Commonwealth marker, reduce the number on the marker by the difference between the commonwealth marker's value and the total gold donated (to a minimum of 1).
> - Give half of the total gold donated, rounded down, to the Leader of the Commonwealth.
> If the Commonwealth has fewer members than the Opposition, no donation takes place. Instead, move the Commonwealth marker 1 step left, stopping at step 1.
> Donated gold is removed from the donors' courts. Apart from the half given to the Leader, the donated gold leaves play. A failed donation leaves the marker on its current step and sets its value to the total donated, to a minimum of 1. When the Opposition outnumbers the Commonwealth, the marker's value does not change.
> This card taps.

## Cautious Schemer | effect card
> Short description: When an event is resolved in [[natural turn]] order, such as the [[Commonwealth Donation]] or [[Council]] event, you may choose to take your turn last.
> Condition: You are eligible to participate in the event under its normal rules.
> Trigger: Before any player takes their turn in an event that uses turn taking in natural order (Commonwealth Donation, Council, Taxation)
> On Trigger: decline or accept
> Effect: move this player to the last spot in the turn taking for this event.

## Overthrow | action card
> Short description: If you have more [[gold]] than the current Leader of your party (Commonwealth or Opposition) you may overthrow them to become the new Leader of that party.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You have more gold than the current Leader of your party.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: You become the Leader of your party, replacing the current Leader.
> Extra rules: When you replace the leader, you don't take ownership of the Cautious Schemer chapter effect card.

## Oppose | action card
> Short description: With the permission of the Leader of your party (Commonwealth or Opposition), or without their permission if you have more [[population]] than your Leader, you may oppose them and become a member of the other party. If this is the first time someone opposes, they become the Opposition Leader. The current Leader of the Opposition or Commonwealth cannot Oppose.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: You are not the Leader of the Commonwealth or Opposition.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card. Then, if you have less or equal population to the leader of your party, they are asked for permission. If they give permission, continue
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: You leave your current party and become a member of the other party. If this is the first time someone opposes, you become the Leader of the Opposition.
> Extra rules: If permission is denied, the selected character/activation card still taps and your turn ends, but you remain in your current party. The Opposition Leader is appointed only when the Opposition is first formed. A member returning to the Commonwealth does not change the Opposition Leader.


## Victory Condition: 
At the end of the Control phase, check these conditions in order. The first condition met by a player who is eligible to win determines the winner; the conditions do not all need to be met.

1. If a player has more gold than all other players combined, then they win.
2. The Leader of the Commonwealth wins when the commonwealth marker is on the last step on the commonwealth track.
3. The Leader of the Opposition wins when the commonwealth marker is on the first step on the commonwealth track.

For the gold comparison, vassals' gold counts towards their Lord rather than separately. The Opposition condition requires an Opposition Leader to exist, but the marker does not need to have advanced first: remaining on its starting step is sufficient.


## Cleanup: 
When this Chapter ends, remove all chapter components.
Return the regular law cards to play.
