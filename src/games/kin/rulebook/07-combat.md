# Combat

[[army|armies]] wage war using the [[Battle]] action. Combat can break [[treaty|treaties]] as stated in the rules of each [[treaty]].

## Dice

When rolling [[combat dice]] for [[Battle]] you can pick between four options in step 4. The Aggresive, Ambush and Defensive dice require you to know their technology before you can use them.

| Side | Strategic | Aggressive | Ambush | Defensive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | nothing | 2 self hits | 2 self hits, 1 hit | 1 hit |
| 2 | 1 hit | 2 self hits, 1 hit | 1 self hit, 1 hit | 1 block |
| 3 | 1 block | 1 self hit, 2 hits | 1 block, 1 hit | 1 block |
| 4 | 1 block, 1 hit | 1 self hit, 2 hits | 2 hits | 1 block, 1 hit |
| 5 | 2 blocks | 2 hits | 1 self hit | 2 blocks |
| 6 | 2 hits | 3 hits | 1 ambush | 3 blocks |
