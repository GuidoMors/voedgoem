Here is the template for what a character should look like:

# Name | title | archetype | x power
> ↷ / ◫ Ability name
> Short description: [cost] - (requirement)  A short description of what the ability does. This is the ruletext that is shown on the card of the character.
> Condition: Conditions to activate the ability. If the Condition is not met, the ability is grey, and will not activate or be clickable.
> Trigger: When the condition is met, the ability can trigger. A trigger can bea  clicking the ruletext for a ↷  ability, or a specific step of an action is reached. If the ability doesn't specify who it applies to, assume it applies to only this player. For instance a Trigger of: Explore, means when you Explore, not when any player uses Explore.
> On trigger: the player might have to select certain resources, armies or other components in play for the ability to resolve. On trigger there is always the option to decline for the player if they want to not resolve the effect of the ability.
> Payment: incurred if ability is activated.
> Effect: The effects of the ability, and possibly any payment for the ability resolves. (if no effect is written, the effect is the same as the short description)
> On decline: What happens if payment is declined.
> Extra Rules: Extra explanation of the ability to provide context.

If one of the fields is empty, that means it has none.
If an ability is a ↷ ability, or a ◫ ability that requires a tap, the condition for that ability is always that the character is untapped.
Abilities can be triggered as long as the character is not a hostage in your court.
