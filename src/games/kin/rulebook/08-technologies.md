# Technologies

Researched with [[gear]]s.

Technologies can be in one of three states:
- Unknown: They are on the research track of the [[technology board]]. Nobody knows them.
- [[claim]]ed: They are in the [[court]] of a player.
- [[public]]: They are on the public track of the [[technology board]]. Everybody knows them.

When you [[Research]] an unknown technology you claim the cards as written on the technology.
For Research the only exception is event cards: they immedaitely go on the event track of the phase board.

When you Publicize a claimed technology you make the cards as written on the technology public.
Public buildings go on the building board. 
Public action cards go on their respective track of the phase board.
