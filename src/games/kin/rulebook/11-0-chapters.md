# Chapters

When the last [[phase]] (the [[control]] phase) ends, the victory condition of the current chapter is checked.
The game starts with the Chapter: New Dawn.

Whenever a new chapter starts, new components and rules are put into play. These are handed out. Then  the [[phase marker]] goes back to the event track.

## Game Types
In a **short game** the winner of the [[New Dawn]] chapter is the winner of the game. No further chapters are played.

In a **long game** the winner of the game is whoever wins two chapters in a row. The winner of a chapter picks the next chapter. They are considered the leader for that chapter. A leader who loses a chapter [[collapse]]s.

The last game type is the **campaign**. For winning a chapter a player scores a campaign point. 
- If a non-leader wins a chapter, they pick the next chapter. They are considered the leader for that chapter. The Kin of the leader of this chapter collapses.
- If the leader wins a chapter, their Kin collapses due to hubris anyway. The next chapter is the New Dawn chapter once again.
The campaign ends when:
- A player collapses and there are no more Kin for them to choose, 
- The leader has to pick a new chapter, but all chapters have already been played.
- The current leader in campaign points has such a large lead, that you can't catch up to them.

## Collapse
When a Kin collapses their player goes through the following:
- Lose everything in court: their hostages go back to their owning Kin, all other resources and cards are discarded.
- Lose everything on the world board: all their armies and buildings are removed. Any roads placed down by them aren't owned by them, so they stay in play. Any tile buildings removed have a [[ruins]] placed on the tile.
- Choose a new Kin: they choose from the unpicked Kin. They then go to the starting setup of that Kin, and choose characters as described in setup.

## The different chapters
1. [[New Dawn]] - The opening chapter: the first to lead 4 out of 6 resources in the winner.
2. [[Commonwealth]] - All players start members of the commonwealth, making decisions for the betterment of all Kinkind. If commonwealth holds, the leader of the commonwealth wins. If the commonwealth is dissolved, its conquerer wins.
3. [[Kingdom]] - If the King enacts enough laws they win. If Vassals rebel and conquer him in a show of force, they win.
4. [[Empire]] - The [[Kin]] vie for control of neutral villages and cities to gain authority. The player with the most authority wins the game.
5. [[Exploration]] - [[Explore]] the [[ruins]] of the world. When all ruins are explored, the winner with the most relics wins.
6. [[Machine Age]] - [[Research]] [[technology|technologies]] until the winner researches the [[Steampower]] technology.
