# New Dawn | Chapter

Color: White

## Rules
This is the starting chapter. As such it has no special addition rules or cards.

## Victory Condition: A player is the winner if they hold the strict most (no ties) in at least four.
- [[Population]] (from buildings on the [[world board]])
- Total [[strength]] (deployed as [[army|armies]] on the [[world board]])
- [[Treaty|Treaties]] (given to others, and in your [[court]])
- [[Mana]] (in your [[court]])
- [[technology|technologies]] (in your [[court]])
- [[Relic]]s (in your [[court]])

Someone who has given out their [[Vassal Treaty]] counts their source to the receiver of their [[treaty]]. [[Tap]]ped and [[untap]]ped cards both count.
