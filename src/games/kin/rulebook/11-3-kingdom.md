# Kingdom | Chapter

Color: dark yellow

## Rules: 
The leader is the King. 
Publicize the [[Vassal Diplomacy]] technology if it isn't already. This chapter does not publicize [[Council Science]].
All players discard their Vassal Ban if they have any.
First dissolve all existing [[Vassal Treaty]] cards without breaking penalties, including any given by the King. Then every player other than the King automatically gives their Vassal Treaty to the King, without Barter, payment, or consent.
Discard the currently enacted law, then shuffle the law deck discard pile into the law deck.

Normal technology requirements for unlocking [[Council]] still apply. If Council has not been unlocked, its event cannot be triggered. Whenever Council is held, the King chooses the enacted law even if another player privately owns Council Science.

Technology-gated laws only enter the law deck when their normal technology requirements are met. Laws that have not yet entered play do not count against the King's victory condition.


## Chapter components: 
The law board: it has two tracks: a rebel track, and a kingdom track.
Cards (public): Rebel, Taxation
Cards (owned by the leader): Righteous Ruler, Iron Grip

## Taxation | event card
> Short description: Every player other than the King pays 1 [[gold]] to the King.
> Effect: In natural order each player other than the King automatically pays 1 gold to the King. If they don't have gold, nothing is paid. No player prompts are needed. This card taps.

## Righteous Ruler | effect card
> Short description: In the [[Council]] event you choose which law is enacted. There is no vote. When you enact a [[law card]], you may choose to place it on the rebel track or kingdom track of the law board. You and your direct [[vassal]]s are immune to the effects of law cards on the rebel track. Law cards on the law board are not discarded during the [[Council]] event.
> 1 - Trigger: Council Event, when the drawn laws are drawn
> 1 - On trigger: select one of the laws. select the rebel track or kingdom track.
> 1 - Effect: skip the vote. Enact the law card: place the law card on the selected track.
> 2 - Condition: there are laws on the rebel track
> 2 - Trigger: when deciding whether an action's conditions are met for a player that is the King or a vassal of the King
> 2 - Effect: You and your direct Vassals are unaffected by the effects of law cards on the rebel track. Indirect vassals and vassals of other players are not immune. Immunity updates immediately when treaty relationships change.
> 3 - Trigger: Council, when discarding law cards.
> 3 - Effect: Law cards on the law board are not discarded.
> Extra rules: Council draws three laws as normal, or as many as are available. Quantum Collapse still chooses which laws are drawn. The King enacts one and discards the others. Grand Works and Influential's reward for voting have no effect because there is no vote. Laws on both tracks remain simultaneously active. Normal Revoke may discard any one law from either track.

## Iron Grip | action card
> Short description: [power equal to the number of law cards on the rebel track]: Enact a [[law card]] from the law card discard pile.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: The law card discard pile is not empty.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card until enough cards with power greater than or equal to the number of law cards on the rebel track are tapped. Select a law card from the law card discard pile. Then select either the rebel track or kingdom track on the law board to place it on.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: Enact the selected law card, and put it on the selected track.
> Extra rules: Tap at least one Control source, even if the rebel track contains no laws. Follow normal multi-card payment rules: enough total power, without unnecessary extra cards.

## Rebel | action card
> Short description: If you have more [[army]] strength than the King, dissolve the [[Vassal Treaty]] the King holds from you.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: Your total army strength is greater than the King's total army strength. The King holds a Vassal Treaty from you.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Dissolve the Vassal Treaty the King holds from you.
> Extra Rules: Your army strength only has to be larger than that of the King, not of any vassals that the King has. The strength of army of Vassals only counts towards victory conditions.
> Only deployed armies on the world board count for this action; armies on the Ark board do not count. Rebel dissolves the treaty without breaking penalties. Enforce Treaties only forbids the Dissolve action card and does not prevent Rebel.

The King may use the normal [[Dissolve]] action to dissolve a subject's Vassal Treaty, subject to normal action restrictions. Players may give new Vassal Treaties through normal [[Barter]] when no Vassal Ban or other normal treaty restriction prevents it.

## Victory Condition: 
At the end of the Control phase, check these conditions in order. The first satisfied condition determines the winner.

1. The King wins when all currently available laws are enacted on the law board: both the law deck and law deck discard pile are empty. Unresearched, technology-locked laws outside these piles do not count.
2. A Rebel (a player who is not a Vassal) wins when their deployed world-board army strength, including their direct and indirect vassals, is strictly greater than the King's and all of the King's direct and indirect vassals combined, and strictly greater than every other Rebel's total. Ties do not win.

A Vassal cannot win. If both conditions are satisfied, the King's condition takes priority.


## Cleanup: 
When this Chapter ends, remove all chapter components.
Return all laws from both tracks to the law discard pile, then shuffle the discard pile into the regular law deck. Public Vassal Diplomacy and existing Vassal Treaties remain in play.
