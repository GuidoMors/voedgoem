# Kingdom | Chapter

## Rules: 
The leader is the King. 
Publicize the [[Vassal Diplomacy]] & [[Council Science]] [technology|technologies]] if they aren't already. 
All players discard their Vassal Ban if they have any.
All players that aren't the King must hand their [[Vassal Treaty]] to the King.
Discard the currently enacted law, then shuffle the law deck discard pile into the law deck.


## Chapter components: 
The law board: it has two tracks: a rebel track, and a kingdom track.
Cards (public): Rebel, Taxation
Cards (owned by the leader): Righteous Ruler, Iron Grip

## Taxation | event card
> Short description: Every player pays 1 [[gold]] to the King.
> Effect: In natural order each player automatically pays 1 gold to the King. If they don't have gold, nothing is paid.

## Righteous Ruler | effect card
> Short description: In the [[Council]] event you choose which law is enacted. There is no vote. When you enact a [[law card]], you may choose to place it on the rebel track or kingdom track of the law board. You and your [[vassal]]s are immune to the effects of law cards on the rebel track. Law cards on the law board are not discarded during the [[Council]] event.
> 1 - Trigger: Council Event, when the drawn laws are drawn
> 1 - On trigger: select one of the laws. select the rebel track or kingdom track.
> 1 - Effect: skip the vote. Enact the law card: place the law card on the selected track.
> 2 - Condition: there are laws on the rebel track
> 2 - Trigger: when deciding whether an action's conditions are met for a player that is the King or a vassal of the King
> 2 - Effect: You and your Vassals are unaffected by the effects of law cards on the rebel track.
> 3 - Trigger: Council, when discarding law cards.
> 3 - Effect: Law cards on the law board are not discarded.

## Iron Grip | action card
> Short description: [power equal to the number of law cards on the rebel track]: Enact a [[law card]] from the law card discard pile.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: The law card discard pile is not empty.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card until enough cards with power greater than or equal to the number of law cards on the rebel track are tapped. Select a law card from the law card discard pile. Then select either the rebel track or kingdom track on the law board to place it on.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation cards.
> Effect: Enact the selected law card, and put it on the selected track.

## Rebel | action card
> Short description: If you have more [[army]] strength than the King, dissolve the [[Vassal Treaty]] the King holds from you.
> Phase: Control
> Condition: Your total army strength is greater than the King's total army strength. The King holds a Vassal Treaty from you.
> On trigger: Select a untapped Control Character/Activation Card.
> Payment: Tapping the selected character/activation card.
> Effect: Dissolve the Vassal Treaty the King holds from you.
> Extra Rules: Your army strength only has to be larger than that of the King, not of any vassals that the King has. The strength of army of Vassals only counts towards victory conditions.

## Victory Condition: 
The King wins when all Laws are enacted on the law board (the law deck and law deck discard pile are empty).
A Rebel (read: non-Vassal) wins when they have a larger strength of army than the King, and all of his vassals combined, and more than any other Rebel.


## Cleanup: 
When this Chapter ends, remove all chapter components.
