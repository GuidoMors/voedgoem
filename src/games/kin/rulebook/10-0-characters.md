# Characters

Each [[character]] has a name, an [[archetype]], a power and an ability.


# Abilities

Every character has an ability. There are 2 types of abilities: ↷ Tap, ◫ Passive: 
- ↷ A tap ability acts like an action card, but it can only be activated by the character that has it by tapping them. After activating the effects of the ability, just like tapping an action card, you end your turn.
- ◫ A passive ability is an ability which does not activate, but affects other actions, like their cost.

Characters with no ability say “No ability”.
