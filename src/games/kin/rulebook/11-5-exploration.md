# Exploration | Chapter

## Rules: 
In natural order each player chooses a tile to place a ruins on until 9 new ruins have been placed. Ruins must be placed on an unbuilt tile without an army on it that is not an ocean.

## Victory Condition: 
The winner is declared when there are no ruins on the world board.
The winner is the player with the most relics. In case of a tie, the winner is the leader of this chapter (even if they don't have the most relics).

## Cleanup: 
When this Chapter ends, each player loses all their relics.
