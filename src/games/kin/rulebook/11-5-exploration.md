# Exploration | Chapter

Color: dark light blue

## Rules:
At the start of this chapter, [[Archeology Science]] becomes public permanently.

Place 9 additional ruins, one at a time, starting with the first Kin in natural order and cycling through that order. Existing ruins remain. Each ruins block must be placed on a non-ocean, unbuilt tile without an army or an existing ruins block on it. Relic and treasure blocks do not prohibit placement. If there are insufficient legal tiles, place as many as possible. No ordinary actions or passing are allowed until this placement is finished.

Archeology, [[Explore]], and [[Reclaim]] continue normally. All ruins on the world board count for this chapter, including any placed later by Archeology.

## Victory Condition:
Check at the end of [[Control]], after initial placement is finished. When ALL ruins on the world board are gone, the eligible player with the most court [[relic]]s wins. Remaining treasure and relic blocks on the world board do not delay victory.

Count all court relics, including preexisting relics and tapped relics. A vassal's relics count towards their ultimate lord through all direct and indirect vassal relationships. Vassals cannot win.

If the most relics are tied, including a tie at zero, the chapter leader wins even if they are not among those tied for most. If the leader is a vassal, their ultimate lord wins instead. A vassal cycle has no eligible winner.

## Cleanup:
When this chapter ends, remove ALL relic resources from every court and reset all tapped relic counts to zero. Leave all world-board blocks in place. Archeology Science stays public.
