# Pride of Lumon
Color: yellow

The Lion-kin of the greater plains were once a vicious Kin, whose men fought each other for a place within their heartless hierarchy, and whose women were delegated to work. But as the disparate groups of the Lion-kin were tamed by the Hanshu, a shift in outlook changed their society to the very core. Although at first loyal subjects of the crown, a fire burned in the hearts of the Lion-kin that could not be quenched. A revolutionary movement of equality and peace called the White Wave overthrew their cruel leaders. At the core of this movement was Lumon; the White Prophet.

Their unshaken faith in their prophet has led them to pacifism and diplomacy, and away from conquering and dictatorship. They see themselves and their beliefs as morally superior, and as such their ways must be spread to the other more ignorant and barbaric factions of the world.

## Starting setup

- Place your starting buildings: 1 village, 1 worksite.
- Place your starting army: Strength 1 on the village.
- Claim the Faction Effects cards and the Faction Building Bluprint.
- Claim the following technologies: Promise Diplomacy, Market Construction.

# Faction Effects

## Plaintsriders | effect card | core
> Moving from [[plains]] to plains costs ½.
// Condition: Army is standing on plains.
// Trigger: Move, when calculating tile speed cost.
// Effect: The tile speed cost for moving to plains is set to ½.

## Virtue | effect card
> [[Virtue]] equals the number of your treaties other players hold. It cannot be spent or bartered. Your characters use it for their abilities.
// Trigger: Barter, when it resolves
// Effect: Set your virtue to the number of your treaties held by other players.
// Extra rules: Virtue is not a bartering good.

## Diplomats | effect card
> When a player accepts a [[treaty]] from you, they receive a gift:
// - 1 [[gear]] for a Peace Treaty, 
// - 1 [[mana]] for an Access Treaty, 
// - 1 [[gold]] for a Trade Treaty.
// Condition: The barter included a peace treaty, access treaty, trade treaty from you.
// Trigger: Barter, when it resolves.
// Effect: The player who accepted the treaty gains 1 gear for a Peace Treaty, and/or 1 mana for an Access Treaty, and/or 1 gold for a Trade Treaty.

## Open Markets | effect card
> You can [[Build]] [[markets]] without an adjacent [[river]].
// Trigger: Build, when checking the Building Conditions for which building blueprints are allowed for that tile.
// Effect: A market does not require an adjacent river to be built.

## The White Prophet | effect card
> You must draft Lumon as the first character in the character draft. Lumon cannot be used as a [[general]], held [[hostage]] by other players, or traded through [[Barter]].
// 1 - Condition: You are drafting characters.
// 1 - Trigger: Character draft, when you draft your first character.
// 1 - Effect: You must draft Lumon as your first character.
// 2 - Condition: Lumon is in your court.
// 2 - Trigger: Whenever a player attempts to use Lumon as a general or take Lumon hostage.
// 2 - Effect: Lumon cannot be used as a general or taken hostage by another player.


# Characters

## Lumon | The White Prophet | Universal | 3 power
> ↷ The White Wave
> Short description: [4 [[mana]]]  - (4 [[virtue]]): Force a player to accept a barter of a selected [[treaty]].
> Condition: You have at least 4 virtue and 4 mana. Can barter at least 1 treaty type.
> Trigger: Click this ability ruletext.
> On trigger: Select a player. Select a Treaty Type from a list of options of all treaties that you can barter to that player.
> Payment: Pay 4 mana. Tap this character.
> Effect: The selected player gains a treaty of the selected type.

## Wololay | Apprentice of the Faith | Discovery | 2 power
> ↷ Turning of Hearts
> Short description: [2 [[mana]]] - (6 [[virtue]]): Transfer command of a lesser [[strength]] [[army]] adjacent to an army you own, if they hold at least 3 of your [[treaty|treaties]].
> Condition: Discovery Phase. You have at least 6 virtue. You own an army with greater strength than an adjacent army on an unbuilt tile owned by another player who holds at least 3 of your treaties.
> Trigger: Click this ability ruletext.
> On trigger: Select an army of lesser strength of another player that is adjacent to an army of greater strength that you own.
> Payment: Pay 2 mana. Tap this character.
> Effect: Transfer the selected army to you.
> Extra Rules: This does not break any treaties.
