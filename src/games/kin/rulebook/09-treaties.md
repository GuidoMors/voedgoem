# Treaties

Treaties already held in a court cannot be traded. In [[Barter]], use **+ Add a treaty** to create a new treaty instead.

A [[treaty]] is held by the receiver. Players may only hold one treaty per Kin, unless stated otherwise.
//“One per Kin” means one treaty of that type from each giver. Dissolving a treaty returns it and does not count as breaking it.

If you break a treaty, that is likely to cause you to gain a Ban card for that treaty: you can never use that treaty in Barter again. You can [[Dissolve]] treaties in your own court without any repurcussions.

## Trade Treaty
> The giver of this treaty [[untap]]s the [[character]] or [[activation card]] used in a succesful [[Barter]].
// Condition: The giver can only barter this treaty with someone they are connected to.
// Breaking: When the receiver or giver attacks the other, they break this treaty.
// Penalty for breaking: No penalty. 

## Access Treaty
> The receiver of an access treaty may pass buildings of the giver, but may not end a [[Move]] on them.
// Breaking: When the receiver or giver attacks the other, they break this treaty.
// Penalty for breaking: Dissolve all Access Treaties the breaker holds, and all Access Treaties the breaker has given out, back to their giver. The breaker may never receive or give an Access Treaty again through Barter.

## Peace Treaty
> Promise to not attack another player for a certain amount of rounds.
// Before barter offer: The giver assigns this treaty a duration in rounds, up to a maximum of 10 rounds. Each Seasons Change it lowers by 1. When the duration reaches 0, the Peace Treaty is dissolved.
// Breaking: When the receiver or giver attacks the other, they break this treaty.
// Penalty for breaking: Dissolve all Peace Treaties the breaker holds, and all Peace Treaties the breaker has given out. Then the breaker gains a Peace Ban card.

## Faction Treaty
> As long as the receiver holds this treaty, they have the [[core faction effect]] of the giver's [[Kin]] available to them in their [[faction effect]]s.
// Breaking: When the receiver or giver attacks the other, they break this treaty.
// Penalty for breaking: No penalty.

## Promise Treaty
> The giver of this treaty writes a promise in plain text on this treaty. The giver assigns another player as the arbiter to decide whether it is upheld each round.
// Breaking: When the arbiter submits that the giver broke the treaty during the Lapsed Treaties event, the giver breaks it.
// Penalty for breaking: Dissolve all Promise Treaties the breaker holds, and all Promise Treaties the breaker has given out. Then the breaker gains a Promise Ban card.
// Extra rules: Other players may hold multiple Promise Treaties of the same Kin at a time.

## Vassal Treaty
> The giver of this treaty becomes a vassal of the receiver (their Lord). As long as the Lord holds this treaty, the vassal cannot win the game. All pieces of the vassal count towards the Lord's victory conditions.
// Condition: No other player has a Vassal Treaty from the giver.
// Breaking: When the receiver or giver attacks the other, they break this treaty.
// Penalty for breaking: Dissolve all Vassal Treaties the breaker holds, and all Vassal Treaties the breaker has given out. Then the breaker gains a Vassal Ban card. If the vassal breaks the treaty, the receiver takes one character of the vassal hostage in their court and takes all of the vassal's gold.
// Extra rules: For victory conditions, the vassal counts as having 0 cards or resources in their court. Their cards and resources count towards their Lord's victory conditions.


# Bans

If you break a Treaty you may end up with a Ban card. All Ban cards work the same: upon acquiring it all treaties of that type that you hold or have given out are dissolved. Then you may never receive or give that treaty for the rest of the game.

## Trade Ban
> Effect: You may no longer give or receive the [[Trade Treaty]].
// Extra rules: this card exists, but is not handed out for breaking a Trade Treaty by default.

## Access Ban
> Effect: You may no longer give or receive the [[Access Treaty]].

## Peace Ban
> Effect: You may no longer give or receive the [[Peace Treaty]].

## Faction Ban
> Effect: You may no longer give or receive the [[Faction Treaty]].
// Extra rules: this card exists, but is not handed out for breaking a Faction Treaty by default.

## Promise Ban
> Effect: You may no longer give or receive the [[Promise Treaty]].

## Vassal Ban
> Effect: You may no longer give or receive the [[Vassal Treaty]].
