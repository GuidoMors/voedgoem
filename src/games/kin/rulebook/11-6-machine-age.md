# Machine Age | Chapter

## Rules: 
In natural order each player moves a technology from the public track to the research track, until 9 such technologies are moved this way, or if no more technologies remain on the public track.

## Research Lost
Research is lost when it is moved from the public track to the research track.
If a Construction technology is lost, its building blueprints are removed, but all the buildings of that type are not removed from the World Board. 
If a Diplomacy technology is lost, that means that the treaty cannot be bartered, but all treaties of that type stay in court. 
If an Science research is lost, the event card is removed from play, but any resources from them (like tactics cards or relics) stay in court.
If an Active research is lost, any activation cards given by them are removed from the courts.


## Victory Condition: 
The winner is the player that researches the Steampower technology.


## Cleanup: 
When this chapter ends, all researches on the public track are lost.
